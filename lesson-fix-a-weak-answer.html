Cat. no. 01 · Lesson 1

Fix a weak answer in three follow-ups

When the reply is too long, too vague or off track, don't start over. Tell the AI exactly what to change.

  • Level: Beginner
  • 6 min read
  • Claude · ChatGPT
  • Updated: 28 September 2026

On this page

  1. First, name what's wrong
  2. The three follow-ups that fix most answers
  3. Show, don't just tell
  4. When to start a new chat
  5. Common mistakes
  6. Try it now: 5 minutes

In this lesson you'll learn

  • Why the first answer is only a first draft
  • Three follow-ups that fix most weak answers
  • When to start a new chat instead

The first answer you get from Claude or ChatGPT is a first draft, not the final result. Most people read it, feel let down, and either give up or type the whole question again. There is a faster way: stay in the same chat and say what to change.

The chat remembers what you've already said, so a short follow-up is often enough. You don't need a perfect prompt. You need to steer.

First, name what's wrong

Before you reply, take five seconds to decide what exactly is wrong. Almost every weak answer has one of five problems, and each has a follow-up that fixes it.

Five common problems and what to reply
What's wrongReply with
Too long"Cut this to 5 bullet points, one line each."
Too vague"Make it specific to my situation: [one sentence about it]. Use a real example."
Wrong tone"Rewrite it so it sounds friendly but professional, like a colleague, not a lawyer."
Missed the point"That's not what I need. I need [X] so that I can [Y]."
Something seems wrong"Which parts are you unsure about? Mark anything I should check."

The three follow-ups that fix most answers

If you remember only three messages, remember these. Each one works in Claude and ChatGPT.

Show, don't just tell

When the style is wrong, describing what you want only goes so far. Paste a short example of something you liked and ask the AI to match it.

Weak prompt

Make it sound better.

Better prompt

Here is an email my team liked last month. Match its tone and length, but use the new details: [paste the example]

Why it works: "Better" means something different to everyone. An example shows exactly what you mean.

Take private details out of examples

If your example is a real work email, change the names, numbers and anything confidential before you paste it. Lesson 5 shows how.

When to start a new chat

Follow-ups work best when the chat is on the right track. Start a fresh chat when:

  • You have sent several corrections and it keeps making the same mistake.
  • You changed topic completely. Details from the old topic can leak into new answers.
  • The conversation has become very long. Carry over only what matters.

Common mistakes

  • Typing the whole question again

    You lose everything the AI already understood. Reply in the same chat and say only what to change.

  • Asking it to "make it better"

    The AI can't read your mind. Say what better means: shorter, warmer, more specific, or in a table.

  • Taking the first answer

    The first reply is a draft. One or two follow-ups are usually the difference between okay and ready to send.

Try it now: 5 minutes

Open Claude or ChatGPT in another tab and follow these steps.

  1. 1Ask for something you need this week in one short sentence, for example: "Write a message asking my team for their holiday dates."
  2. 2Read the answer and name its biggest problem using the table above.
  3. 3Send the matching reply. Then send follow-up 3 and answer its questions.
  4. 4Compare the final answer with the first one, and notice how few words it took.
