2Room 2: Room 2 · The Four Parts

Lesson 0: The 4-part prompt

Most weak answers come from short, vague questions. Before you press send, check that your prompt has these four parts.

The four parts

  1. 1RoleWho should the AI act as?
  2. 2TaskWhat exactly do you want?
  3. 3ContextWhat does it need to know that it can't guess?
  4. 4FormatHow should the answer look? Length, shape, tone.

Pick an example

Build it yourself

Tap a part to take it out, and see what the AI would have to guess.

Your prompt

You're my office assistant. Write a short email to my team saying Thursday's meeting moves to 2 pm. There are 6 of us and some work remotely. Keep it under 80 words and end with a question.

All four parts are in. The AI has what it needs.

Example answer

 

Subject: Thursday's meeting moves to 2 pm Hi team, Quick update: Thursday's meeting now starts at 2 pm. Same link for anyone joining remotely. Thanks! Can everyone make the new time?

Written in advance to show the difference. Real answers will vary.

Why it works: The AI now knows who it is writing to, what changed, and how long the email should be.

Works the same in Claude and ChatGPT. Paste it, press send, and compare the answer with the weak version.

8Room 8: Room 8 · Information Desk

Questions visitors ask

Do I need a technical background?

No. The lessons use everyday language and real examples. If you can write an email, you can follow along.

Claude or ChatGPT: which one should I use?

Start with the one you already have. Most lessons work in both, and I point out when something is different.

Are the lessons free?

Yes. The lessons on this site are free to read.

Is the site available in Arabic?

Yes. Use the “العربية” button at the top of the page to switch languages.

Is it safe to paste my work files into AI?

Be careful. Don't paste passwords, ID numbers, or confidential company data unless your company allows it. Check your workplace's AI policy first. Lesson 5, “Use AI safely at work”, covers this in detail.

Are you connected to Anthropic or OpenAI?

No. This is an independent site. I teach people how to use their tools.

Who made the artworks?

Every artwork on this site is drawn in code: the engraved portrait, the prints, the watercolour and the lamps. Nothing here is stock art.
