Before anything is sent:</p><ol class="steps"><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">1</span><span>Ask the AI: "List every number in your notes and the row of my table it came from. Flag any number that is not in my table."</span></li><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">2</span><span>Tie every number in the draft to the ledger or your working file, and tick it off.</span></li><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">3</span><span>Recompute totals and differences in Excel yourself.</span></li><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">4</span><span>Delete any reason you did not give the AI, and check the period, dates and account names.</span></li></ol><aside class="callout callout--warn"><p class="callout__title">Keep real ledgers out of public AI tools</p><p>Never paste real ledgers, customer names, salaries or bank details into a public AI tool. Use labels like Customer A, round the numbers, or use the tool your company has approved. Lesson 5 shows how.</p></aside></section><section id="mistakes" class="lesson-section" aria-labelledby="mistakes-h"><h2 id="mistakes-h" class="lesson-h2">Common mistakes</h2><ul class="mistakes" role="list"><li class="mistakes__item"><p class="mistakes__title">Letting the AI guess the reasons</p><p>Numbers alone get invented reasons that sound right. Give it your reasons, and ask it to mark anything missing.</p></li><li class="mistakes__item"><p class="mistakes__title">Trusting the AI's arithmetic</p><p>AI can add up a column wrong and still sound sure. Recompute every total and every difference in Excel.</p></li><li class="mistakes__item"><p class="mistakes__title">Pasting the real ledger</p><p>A ledger export carries customer names, supplier names and amounts. Use made-up or rounded numbers, or a tool your company has approved.</p></li></ul></section><section id="practice" class="lesson-section practice" aria-labelledby="practice-h"><h2 id="practice-h" class="lesson-h2">Try it now: 5 minutes</h2><p>Try it with a real task from your week.
