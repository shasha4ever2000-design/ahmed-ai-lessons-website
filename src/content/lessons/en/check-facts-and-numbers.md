They can invent a statistic, a quote, a rule or a source that sounds completely real. This is often called "hallucination". Newer tools make fewer of these mistakes, but none make zero.</p><p class="prose__intro">You don't need to distrust everything. You need a quick routine for the answers that matter: numbers, names, dates, rules, and anything you'll send to someone else.</p></div><section id="why" class="prose lesson-section" aria-labelledby="why-h"><h2 id="why-h" class="lesson-h2">Why confident doesn't mean correct</h2><p>An AI model writes the most likely next words, based on patterns it learned. It doesn't look things up unless it is searching the web or reading a file you gave it. So a smooth, detailed answer can still contain a made-up detail.</p><ul class="prose-list"><li>Riskiest: specific numbers, dates, prices, names, laws and rules, quotes, and sources.</li><li>Also risky: anything recent. The AI's knowledge stops at a cut-off date unless it searches the web.</li><li>Usually safer: rewriting your own text, explaining a general idea, brainstorming.</li></ul></section><section id="five-checks" class="prose lesson-section" aria-labelledby="five-checks-h"><h2 id="five-checks-h" class="lesson-h2">Five quick checks</h2><ol class="steps"><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">1</span><span>Ask where each fact came from, then open the source yourself.</span></li><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">2</span><span>Recalculate key numbers with a calculator or in Excel.</span></li><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">3</span><span>Check names, dates and titles against an official page.</span></li><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">4</span><span>For laws, tax and company rules, confirm with the official source or an expert. Rules change, and details depend on your country.</span></li><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">5</span><span>Ask the AI to review its own answer (prompt below), then check what it flags.</span></li></ol><figure class="sheet prompt-card prompt-card--plain"><div class="sheet__head"><figcaption class="sheet__label">Self-check prompt</figcaption><button type="button" class="btn btn--quiet btn--sm copy-btn" aria-label="Copy: Self-check prompt"><svg width="18" height="18" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="1.8" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false" class="icon"><rect x="9" y="9" width="11" height="11" rx="2"></rect><path d="M5 15V6a2 2 0 0 1 2-2h9"></path></svg>Copy</button><span class="copy-status visually-hidden" role="status" aria-live="polite"></span></div><p class="sheet__text" dir="auto">Review your last answer. List every fact, number and date in it, and mark each one as 'sure', 'unsure' or 'needs checking'. Tell me how I could verify the unsure ones.</p></figure></section><section id="numbers" class="prose lesson-section" aria-labelledby="numbers-h"><h2 id="numbers-h" class="lesson-h2">Numbers need extra care</h2><div class="compare"><div class="compare__col compare__col--weak"><p class="compare__label">Weak prompt</p><p class="compare__text" dir="auto">What's the total and the average?</p></div><div class="compare__col compare__col--strong"><div class="compare__head"><p class="compare__label">Better prompt</p><button type="button" class="btn btn--quiet btn--sm copy-btn" aria-label="Copy: Better prompt"><svg width="18" height="18" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="1.8" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false" class="icon"><rect x="9" y="9" width="11" height="11" rx="2"></rect><path d="M5 15V6a2 2 0 0 1 2-2h9"></path></svg>Copy</button><span class="copy-status visually-hidden" role="status" aria-live="polite"></span></div><p class="compare__text" dir="auto">Calculate the total and the average of these 12 values. Show each step, then give the final numbers in a small table so I can check them.</p></div><p class="compare__why"><strong>Why it works:</strong> Asking for the steps makes mistakes visible. You can check each line instead of trusting one number.</p></div><aside class="callout callout--tip"><p class="callout__title">Let the spreadsheet do the maths</p><p>For anything important, ask the AI for the Excel formula instead of the result. Excel calculates; the AI explains.</p></aside></section><section id="sources" class="prose lesson-section" aria-labelledby="sources-h"><h2 id="sources-h" class="lesson-h2">Sources that don't exist</h2><p>AI can produce references that look real but aren't: a report title, an article, a link. Before you quote anything:</p><ul class="prose-list"><li>Open every link. If it doesn't load, or says something else, don't use it.</li><li>Search for the exact title of any report or article.</li><li>Prefer official sources: government sites, the company's own page, the original research.</li></ul><aside class="callout callout--warn"><p class="callout__title">Never pass on a source you haven't opened</p><p>If you can't find the source, treat the fact as unverified.</p></aside></section><section id="mistakes" class="lesson-section" aria-labelledby="mistakes-h"><h2 id="mistakes-h" class="lesson-h2">Common mistakes</h2><ul class="mistakes" role="list"><li class="mistakes__item"><p class="mistakes__title">Trusting an answer because it's detailed</p><p>Detail is not proof. Invented facts often come with confident detail.</p></li><li class="mistakes__item"><p class="mistakes__title">Checking only the easy parts</p><p>Check the parts that would cause harm if wrong: numbers, names, dates and rules.</p></li><li class="mistakes__item"><p class="mistakes__title">Asking "are you sure?" and stopping there</p><p>The AI may simply agree, or change its answer. Check an outside source.</p></li></ul></section><section id="practice" class="lesson-section practice" aria-labelledby="practice-h"><h2 id="practice-h" class="lesson-h2">Try it now: 5 minutes</h2><p>Try it with a real task from your week. Keep this page open in another tab.</p><ol class="steps"><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">1</span><span>Ask a factual question about your field that you already know the answer to.</span></li><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">2</span><span>Send the self-check prompt above.</span></li><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">3</span><span>Pick one fact it marked as 'sure' and check it in an official source.</span></li><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">4</span><span>Notice which kinds of details needed checking, and check those first next time.</span></li></ol></section><figure class="takeaway"><p class="small-title">Remember this</p><blockquote><p>Use AI to draft and explain. Before you rely on a fact or a number, check it somewhere outside the chat.</p></blockquote></figure>
