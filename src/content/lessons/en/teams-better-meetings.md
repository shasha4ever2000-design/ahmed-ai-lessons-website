Point out anything that could be an email."</p></aside></section><section id="during" class="prose lesson-section" aria-labelledby="during-h"><h2 id="during-h" class="lesson-h2">During: notes and the meeting chat</h2><p>Write things down in the meeting, not from memory afterwards. Both tools are in the meeting controls.</p><ul class="prose-list"><li>Notes: the shared agenda, notes and tasks. Attendees from your organization can edit them live. External attendees can't open them.</li><li>Chat: for links, files and quick questions, so the talk keeps moving. Invitees can use it before, during and after the meeting.</li></ul><aside class="callout callout--tip"><p class="callout__title">Write decisions as you go</p><p>When something is agreed, type it in Notes straight away ("Decision: the pilot starts with the finance team") and read it out. The recap then almost writes itself.</p></aside></section><section id="record" class="prose lesson-section" aria-labelledby="record-h"><h2 id="record-h" class="lesson-h2">Recording and transcripts: ask first</h2><p>A recording helps people who missed the meeting, and the AI features in Teams need a transcript. But people speak less freely when recorded, so make it a choice, not a habit.</p><ol class="steps"><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">1</span><span>Ask first: "Can I record and transcribe this so Khalid can catch up?"</span></li><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">2</span><span>Select More actions → Record and transcribe → Start recording. Transcription starts with it. For text only, choose Start transcription.</span></li><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">3</span><span>Afterwards, the recording appears in the meeting chat, and the transcript is under Recap → Transcript.</span></li></ol><p>The organizer and people from the same organization can start a recording. Guests and people from other companies can't. If the button is missing, the organizer or IT has probably limited it.</p><aside class="callout callout--warn"><p class="callout__title">Permission comes first</p><p>Teams shows everyone a notice when recording starts, but Microsoft notes that, depending on your region, you may need everyone's permission first. If anyone says no, take notes instead.</p></aside></section><section id="recap" class="prose lesson-section" aria-labelledby="recap-h"><h2 id="recap-h" class="lesson-h2">After: share a recap with decisions and owners</h2><p>The recap is what people act on.
