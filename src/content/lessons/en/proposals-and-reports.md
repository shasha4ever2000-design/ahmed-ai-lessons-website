Then ask for one section at a time, so you can check each part as you go.</p></section><section id="your-voice" class="prose lesson-section" aria-labelledby="your-voice-h"><h2 id="your-voice-h" class="lesson-h2">Write in your voice</h2><p>Paste a short sample of your own writing, like an email or a report you're happy with, and ask the AI to describe your style before it writes.</p><figure class="sheet prompt-card prompt-card--plain"><div class="sheet__head"><figcaption class="sheet__label">Style sample prompt</figcaption><button type="button" class="btn btn--quiet btn--sm copy-btn" aria-label="Copy: Style sample prompt"><svg width="18" height="18" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="1.8" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false" class="icon"><rect x="9" y="9" width="11" height="11" rx="2"></rect><path d="M5 15V6a2 2 0 0 1 2-2h9"></path></svg>Copy</button><span class="copy-status visually-hidden" role="status" aria-live="polite"></span></div><p class="sheet__text" dir="auto">Here is a sample of how I write. Paste them into future prompts, or into a ChatGPT or Claude project.</p></aside></section><section id="ai-smell" class="prose lesson-section" aria-labelledby="ai-smell-h"><h2 id="ai-smell-h" class="lesson-h2">Remove the "AI smell"</h2><p>Readers spot AI writing quickly now.
