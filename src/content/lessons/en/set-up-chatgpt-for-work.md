They work on every plan, and a change applies to your chats straight away.</p><p>Good instructions answer two questions: what should ChatGPT know about you, and how should it answer? Anything you would otherwise repeat in most chats belongs here. Projects are on every plan; paid plans allow more files per project.</p><ul class="prose-list"><li>Monthly report: last month's report as an example, plus the structure you always use.</li><li>Customer replies: your tone guide and a few approved answers, with names removed.</li><li>Learning a topic: your notes and the articles you are reading.</li></ul><figure class="sheet prompt-card prompt-card--plain"><div class="sheet__head"><figcaption class="sheet__label">Project instructions template</figcaption><button type="button" class="btn btn--quiet btn--sm copy-btn" aria-label="Copy: Project instructions template"><svg width="18" height="18" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="1.8" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false" class="icon"><rect x="9" y="9" width="11" height="11" rx="2"></rect><path d="M5 15V6a2 2 0 0 1 2-2h9"></path></svg>Copy</button><span class="copy-status visually-hidden" role="status" aria-live="polite"></span></div><p class="sheet__text" dir="auto">This project is for [job]. Deleting a project deletes its chats and the files stored only in it, so keep your own copies.</p></aside></section><section id="memory" class="prose lesson-section" aria-labelledby="memory-h"><h2 id="memory-h" class="lesson-h2">Memory: know what it keeps</h2><p>ChatGPT can carry useful details from one chat to the next in two ways: saved memories (facts it decided to keep, like "prefers short answers") and references to your past chats. Both are in Settings → Personalization → Memory, where you can see what it kept, delete items, or switch memory off.</p><ul class="prose-list"><li>Look at the list now and then, and delete anything wrong or out of date.</li><li>Deleting a chat does not delete a memory that came from it.
