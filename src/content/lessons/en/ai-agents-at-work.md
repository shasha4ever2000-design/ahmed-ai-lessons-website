An agent acts. You give it a goal and it works through the steps itself: opening web pages, clicking, filling in forms, collecting information and producing a file at the end.</p><p class="prose__intro">Agents are useful and improving fast, but they make mistakes and they can be tricked. Instead of telling you how to compare three suppliers, it opens their websites, reads them and builds the comparison.</p><p>In September 2026 the best-known examples are ChatGPT Work (which replaced ChatGPT agent), Claude in Chrome (a browser extension on paid Claude plans) and computer use in Claude Cowork, which can work in apps on your computer and is still in beta. Names and plans change often, so check the vendor's help page before you start.</p><aside class="callout callout--tip"><p class="callout__title">A simple test</p><p>If the AI only writes text for you, it is a chatbot. "Handle my inbox" is too broad; "find three venues and compare them" is right.</span></li><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">2</span><span>Give it only the access it needs. Turn off connected apps and sites the task doesn't use.</span></li><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">3</span><span>Stay nearby and watch. Take over yourself for sign-ins and anything sensitive.</span></li><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">4</span><span>Keep approvals on for important actions. Both OpenAI and Anthropic build in confirmation steps, so don't switch them off.</span></li><li><span class="steps__n" aria-hidden="true">5</span><span>Stop it if anything looks odd, like visiting sites you didn't expect or asking for information it shouldn't need.</span></li></ol><aside class="callout callout--warn"><p class="callout__title">Follow your company's rules</p><p>Many companies have not approved agents for work accounts yet. Ask before you connect one to your work email, files or systems. You stay responsible for what it does in your name.</p></aside></section><section id="mistakes" class="lesson-section" aria-labelledby="mistakes-h"><h2 id="mistakes-h" class="lesson-h2">Common mistakes</h2><ul class="mistakes" role="list"><li class="mistakes__item"><p class="mistakes__title">Giving it a vague goal</p><p>Vague goals lead to wandering and surprises. Say exactly what to do, which sites to use and what the result should look like.</p></li><li class="mistakes__item"><p class="mistakes__title">Leaving it alone while it is signed in</p><p>An agent that is signed in to your accounts can act as you. Watch it while it works there.</p></li><li class="mistakes__item"><p class="mistakes__title">Trusting the final file without checking</p><p>Agents copy things wrongly and skip steps. Check the key facts, as you would with any AI answer (Lesson 6).</p></li></ul></section><section id="practice" class="lesson-section practice" aria-labelledby="practice-h"><h2 id="practice-h" class="lesson-h2">Try it now: 5 minutes</h2><p>Try it with a real task from your week.
