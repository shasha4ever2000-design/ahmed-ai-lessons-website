Split or summarise very large documents first.</li><li>Each project keeps its own memory, separate from your other chats. You can view, pause or reset memory in Settings.</li><li>On Team and Enterprise plans you can share a project with colleagues, as view-only or with editing.</li></ul><aside class="callout callout--warn"><p class="callout__title">Check the training setting</p><p>On personal plans (Free, Pro and Max) you choose whether your chats can be used to improve Claude: see Settings → Privacy. Team and Enterprise chats are not used for training by default. Either way, keep passwords, customers' personal data and confidential numbers out of project files. Lesson 5 explains why.</p></aside></section><section id="mistakes" class="lesson-section" aria-labelledby="mistakes-h"><h2 id="mistakes-h" class="lesson-h2">Common mistakes</h2><ul class="mistakes" role="list"><li class="mistakes__item"><p class="mistakes__title">Adding every file you have</p><p>More is not better. Old or conflicting files confuse the answers. Add only what the job needs.</p></li><li class="mistakes__item"><p class="mistakes__title">Leaving the instructions empty</p><p>Files tell Claude what to know. Instructions tell it how to work. You need both.</p></li><li class="mistakes__item"><p class="mistakes__title">Never updating the project</p><p>Projects go stale. Put a monthly reminder in your calendar to review the files.</p></li></ul></section><section id="practice" class="lesson-section practice" aria-labelledby="practice-h"><h2 id="practice-h" class="lesson-h2">Try it now: 5 minutes</h2><p>Try it with a real task from your week.
